// Explained

Two Different Simulation Tracks

The safest way to think about Pragmata's sim content is that the game has one Shelter training track and one post-game Hidden Chamber track. They connect to each other in completion routing, but current sources describe them as different systems with different unlock logic.

// Unlock Logic

How Shelter Training Sims Unlock

1. Shelter Progression

Current trophy-roadmap coverage describes Shelter Training Sims as a staged unlock path tied to your ongoing Shelter growth. The stable overlap is that more sims appear as you clear bosses and move the story forward.

2. Training Data Matters

Training Data is the collectible that most directly expands this system. Current guide coverage treats it as one of the keys to opening more Shelter simulation content, which is why the Training Data page is the most natural companion to this one.

Clean phrasing: Training Data supports more Shelter Training Sim unlocks. It is not the same thing as Hidden Chamber pod access.

3. Trophy Requirement

The highest-confidence trophy wording is simple: complete the main objective in one Training Sim for Mister Simulator, then complete the main objective in every Training Sim for Master of the Simulator.

Important simplifier: current trophy guides agree the bonus or side objectives are not required for the Simulator trophies.

// Stable Facts

What Is Safe To Say Right Now

Documented Shelter Count

Current trophy coverage supports a total of 30 Shelter Training Sim missions. That number is useful for checklist planning, but it is also the fact on this page that most deserves a quick future recheck after any balance or content update.

Post-Game Count

Unknown Signal is currently documented as adding 10 Hidden Chamber missions or pods. Multiple current post-game guides agree on that number and on the mode's role as the real cleanup-and-challenge route after credits.

NG+ Carryover Relevance

Current NG+ coverage agrees that one-time collectibles such as Training Data and Storage Expanders stay collected. That is one more reason to finish your sim-related cleanup in Unknown Signal before treating NG+ as a replay file.

Best Practical Framing

If you are unsure what to do next, think of the route as: story progress -> Training Data and Shelter sims -> post-game Hidden Chamber -> ending cleanup. That order matches the strongest current overlap between trophy and post-game guides.

// Best Route

Safest Completion Route

1. Do Story-Era Sim Work Naturally

Let Shelter upgrades and normal Training Data pickups open the first chunk of Training Sims during the main run instead of forcing a dedicated sim grind too early.

2. Track Training Data Carefully

If your Shelter sim list feels thin, the first page to check is Training Data, not Hidden Chamber.

3. Use Unknown Signal For Cleanup

Once credits roll, switch into Unknown Signal first. This is the cleanest place to finish collectible leftovers and separate post-game Sim Pod progress from the Shelter checklist.

4. Save NG+ For Replay Value

NG+ is best used after your simulation and completion route is stable, because current sources treat it as a replay/build-maxing mode rather than the main cleanup layer.

Most common confusion: players often assume that “sim missions” in the Hidden Chamber are the same checklist as Shelter Training Sims. Current post-game and trophy coverage does not support that. Treat them as neighboring systems, not one combined menu.

// FAQ

Training Simulations FAQ

How many Training Simulations are in Pragmata? +

Current trophy-guide coverage supports 30 Shelter Training Sim missions. Separate from that, Unknown Signal adds 10 Hidden Chamber missions in the post-game.

What does Training Data do in Pragmata? +

Current coverage treats Training Data as a one-time collectible that helps unlock more Shelter Training Simulation content.

Do side objectives matter for Master of the Simulator? +

Current trophy guides agree that only the main objective matters for Mister Simulator and Master of the Simulator, not the side objectives.

Are Hidden Chamber Sim Pods the same as Shelter Training Sims? +

No. Current post-game guides describe Hidden Chamber Sim Pods as a separate post-game mission track inside Unknown Signal, while Training Sims refer to the Shelter system.
