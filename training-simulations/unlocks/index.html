// Shelter Levels

Shelter Levels Explain The Main Unlock Bands

The most stable public explanation comes from the Shelter side of the system: once the Cabin and Shelter upgrade, the Training Sim board opens in larger batches instead of one tiny mission at a time.

Shelter Level 2

Unlocks the first wave of Training Sims, covering missions 1 to 10.

Shelter Level 3

Adds the second wave, expanding the list through 11 to 20.

Shelter Level 4

Opens the last batch, completing the full 30-sim board.

Practical takeaway: if your goal is simply to see more sims appear, treat Shelter progression as the first lever and Training Data as the support lever.

What Each Training Data Unlocks

The Honest Answer

Public guide coverage agrees that Training Data matters for unlocking more Shelter sim content, but it does not consistently document an exact one-to-one menu mapping for each of the six pickups.

If you see a page claiming that one specific Training Data collectible definitively unlocks one specific sim by itself, treat that as low-confidence unless it is backed by current video or menu-proof evidence.

Best Safe Framing

  • Shelter upgrades appear to control the big unlock bands.
  • Training Data appears to expand or support the available sim pool within that system.
  • All 6 Training Data still matter if your goal is a complete Shelter sim file.

// Best First Sims

Best Sims To Open First

The list below is an editorial priority order built on confirmed mission names and objectives. It is not claiming these are the only good sims. It is the cleanest first-open route if you want movement, hacking, combat fundamentals, and easy trophy progress early.

1. Lightning Speed

Start With Movement

Lightning Speed is the most natural first sim because it teaches a clean movement check without asking for heavy system knowledge.

Best first pick if you want a low-friction warm-up and quick board momentum.

2. Medic

Learn Drone Handling

Medic is a strong early open because it forces you to interact with the Reassembler Drone loop instead of treating it as side clutter.

Best early pick if you want safer combat rhythm and cleaner objective handling.

3. Project Pierce

Open A Real Combat Tool

Project Pierce is a great early combat sim because it revolves around the Charge Piercer, which makes its value easier to feel outside the sim board.

Best early pick if you want a sim that immediately feeds back into real combat comfort.

4. Locksmith

Then Clean Up Hacking

Locksmith is the best early utility sim if your run still feels messy around hacking pressure, routing speed, or Diana-side execution.

Best early pick if you want more confidence in hacking-heavy cleanup routes.

// FAQ

Training Sim Unlocks FAQ

How do Training Sims unlock in Pragmata? +

Current guide coverage consistently ties Shelter Training Sims to Shelter progression, while other current sources also connect Training Data to expanding the available sim pool. The exact one-to-one Training Data mapping is not well documented publicly.

How many Shelter Training Sims are there? +

Current trophy and simulation guide coverage supports a total of 30 Shelter Training Sim missions.

Which Training Sims should I open first? +

A practical first group is Lightning Speed, Medic, Project Pierce, and Locksmith because they cover movement, drone handling, Charge Piercer fundamentals, and hacking cleanup with low route friction.

Do side objectives matter for Master of the Simulator? +

Current trophy coverage says the main objective is what matters for Mister Simulator and Master of the Simulator, not the side objectives.
