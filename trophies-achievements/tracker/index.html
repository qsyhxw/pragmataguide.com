// FAQ

Trophy Tracker FAQ

How many trophies does this page track? +
It tracks the 35 shared achievements used on Steam and similar platform-neutral lists, with an optional PlayStation Platinum layer that raises the PS5 total to 36 trophies.
What should I prioritize on the first run? +
Story milestones take care of themselves, so the real first-run priority is not forgetting You're Not Getting Away That Easy while Sweeper bot opportunities are still convenient.
Does Unknown Signal matter for the tracker? +
Very much. Current completion and ending coverage ties Unknown Signal to cleanup-friendly routing, Hidden Chamber mission work, and The Right Man for the Job.
Why does Lunatic get its own phase filter? +
Because the strongest roadmap overlap still treats Lunatic as a separate second-run requirement rather than something you naturally complete during the first story file.
