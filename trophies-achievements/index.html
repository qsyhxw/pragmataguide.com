// Recommended Order

Safe Trophy Roadmap

1. Story Run + Natural Cleanup

Use the first playthrough to clear the story trophies, print as much gear as possible, unlock Shelter systems, and clean up easy combat or collectible milestones while the route is still fresh.

The safest first-run companion pages are All Collectibles, Walkthrough, and Boss Guides.

2. Unknown Signal + 100% Systems

This is the cleanup-heavy phase: sector percentages, special sector missions, stronger boss revisits, and the mode most trophy roadmaps tie to the real end of the completion route.

If you skipped Safe Boxes, Pure Lunum, Red Zones, or Mini Cabins earlier, this is where those omissions start affecting The Right Man for the Job.

3. Lunatic Difficulty

Leave the hardest difficulty for last unless you already know the route. By then you should understand boss timings, matrix pressure, and which loadouts reduce late-run volatility.

Current coverage is consistent that Lunatic is the run that usually decides whether your Platinum or 100% push stays efficient.

// Watch List

High-Risk Trophies

You're Not Getting Away That Easy
This is the one current roadmap coverage most clearly treats as missable. The problem is not the requirement itself; it is the limited and somewhat inconsistent Sweeper bot spawn coverage. If you care about clean completion, do not leave this one for “later.”
The Right Man for the Job
Unknown Signal is not just a bonus mode. Stronger guides treat it as the post-story completion route that pulls together collectibles, pod clears, and the game's last serious cleanup layer.
Can't Stop, Won't Stop
This is safer if you keep your Unit Printer planning clean during the main story instead of assuming post-game will cover every printable requirement.
Master of the Simulator
Training Sim work is time-consuming rather than deceptive. Treat it like a structured checklist task, not a combat challenge you randomly mop up late.

// FAQ

Trophies FAQ

How many trophies and achievements are in Pragmata? +

Current launch-week tracking shows 36 trophies on PlayStation 5 including Platinum, and 35 achievements on Steam without a Platinum equivalent.

Is anything missable? +

Most cleanup-heavy trophies are post-story friendly, but stronger roadmap coverage still flags You're Not Getting Away That Easy as the one meaningful missable-risk trophy because Sweeper bot spawns are limited.

Do you need two playthroughs? +

Yes, that is still the safest assumption. Current trophy roadmaps consistently tie Lunar Supremacy to a Lunatic clear, and Lunatic is treated as a post-first-clear unlock.

Does Unknown Signal matter for full completion? +

Very much so. Current completion coverage treats Unknown Signal as the mode that ties together post-story cleanup, special sector missions, and one of the list's biggest endgame milestones.
