Pragmata Mini Cabin Guide

Mini Cabins are one of the easiest collectible sets to overlook in Pragmata because they do not behave like the rest of the item pool. They are hidden audio-based figures, they require you to shoot them rather than pick them up, and neither the sector completion tracker nor Diana’s scan does a good job helping you find them. This page keeps to the highest-confidence rules and route advice.

// Rules

How Mini Cabins Actually Work

You Shoot Them

Mini Cabins do not behave like pickups on the floor. The most stable cross-source rule is simple: find the figure and shoot it. If you only walk up to one, it will not count.

Fast reminder: any weapon works. This is a recognition test, not a loadout test.

Audio Matters More Than Vision

The chime or `ta-da` sound is the most consistent clue. Because many figures sit on ledges, fans, roofs, or pipes, the right reaction is usually not “keep running,” but “stop and look up, back, and sideways.”

Why people miss them: the sound can bounce strangely in vertical rooms, especially in Mass Production Array and Lunum Mines.

// Completion Rules

What Mini Cabins Do Not Count For

Not Part Of Normal Sector 100%

Multiple Mini Cabin guides agree that these figures sit outside the standard sector completion tracker. That is why a “100% sector” file can still be missing Mini Cabins.

Safe assumption: treat Mini Cabins as their own collectible project, not as a byproduct of normal completion.

Diana’s Scan Will Not Save You

High-confidence third-party coverage repeatedly warns that Mini Cabins are not revealed by Diana’s collectible scan behavior. If you are relying on scanner cleanup logic, this is the one collectible class most likely to slip through the cracks.

Practical reading: when hunting Mini Cabins, trust your ears and your camera, not your normal collectible habits.

// Reward

Why Collect All 15

Mini-Hunter Supreme

The most stable reward claim is that collecting all 15 Mini Cabins unlocks Mini-Hunter Supreme on the platform achievement / trophy side. Current guide coverage does not support any major gameplay reward beyond that.

No Real Build Reward

If you are routing purely for efficiency, Mini Cabins are optional until you decide to trophy clean up. That is exactly why many players postpone them until a dedicated pass.

// Best Route

Safest Mini Cabin Route

During The Story

  1. Grab the obvious ones when the chime is unmistakable.
  2. Do not waste too much time if the sound source is vertical or unclear.
  3. Mentally mark suspicious fans, pipes, ledges, and mesh-covered bunks.
  4. Keep moving unless the figure is clearly on-path.

Cleanup Pass

The safer completionist plan is still a dedicated cleanup pass after the story, especially through Unknown Signal Mode. Current guide coverage supports that none of the Mini Cabins are permanently missable, so there is little reason to stress over every one on a blind first run.

Best use case: do a single audio-focused run once your route anxiety is gone and the main game is finished.
