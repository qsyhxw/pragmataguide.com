Taken together, these three item families form the most important permanent-growth cluster currently documented in Pragmata. // Hugh Focus

What Improves Hugh

The direct Hugh-side permanent upgrade is the Cartridge Holder. Each one adds an extra Repair Cartridge slot, which means more healing inventory and less punishment when you are clearing large combat stretches or learning a new boss pattern.

Because two Cartridge Holders are field pickups and four more come from Cabin's Stamp Club, Hugh's survivability curve depends on both map discipline and Shelter economy discipline. This is one of the rare permanent systems where the field route and the Stamp Club route really cannot be separated cleanly.

Best early value: Cartridge Holders are usually the most immediately felt permanent upgrade on a first run, because extra healing capacity pays off even if your build is still messy.

// Diana Focus

What Improves Diana

Storage Expanders are the direct Diana-side permanent upgrade. Current guide coverage agrees they increase Diana's Hacking Gauge capacity, which makes longer or less efficient hacking strings more forgiving.

The practical effect is less about raw damage and more about flexibility. A bigger gauge gives you more room when a fight asks for quick adaptation, when a hack path gets awkward, or when you simply want a wider comfort margin.

Useful distinction: Storage Expanders are the cleanest example of a permanent upgrade that helps Diana directly, while Training Data helps the Shelter systems around her instead of changing a single combat stat on pickup.

// Shared Progression

Training Data As A Bridge System

Training Data is the least direct of the three permanent-growth categories, but it still belongs in this cluster. It does not raise Hugh's healing limit or Diana's gauge on the spot. Instead, it unlocks additional Training Simulation content at the Shelter, which feeds back into broader progression, rewards, and trophy cleanup.

That makes Training Data the most future-facing permanent upgrade type. If Cartridge Holders help you survive the present and Storage Expanders help you hack more comfortably right now, Training Data improves the quality and breadth of what your Shelter loop can offer over time.

// Route Logic

Best Upgrade Pickup Order

1. Prioritize Early Hugh Value

For most first-run players, the strongest early value comes from field Cartridge Holders and easy Stamp Club progress toward later holders. Extra healing capacity stabilizes almost everything else.

Why first: survivability pays back immediately, even before you optimize your hacking or your Shelter loop.

2. Fold In Diana Comfort

Once your route reaches the first realistic Storage Expander windows, start treating them as high-value detours. They do not always feel as dramatic as a healing-slot upgrade, but they make tougher hacks much less brittle.

Best pairing: collect Storage Expanders alongside Red Zone or late-backtrack cleanup rather than as isolated errands.

3. Keep Training Data On Schedule

Training Data is the easiest upgrade family to postpone too long because its value is indirect. The safer habit is to keep pace once Terra Dome starts the chain, so your Shelter sim options do not lag behind the campaign.

Common mistake: treating Training Data like disposable side loot and then wondering why Shelter sim progression feels thin later.
