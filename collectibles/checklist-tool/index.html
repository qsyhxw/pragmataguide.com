// Why This Tool Exists

Built For Cleanup Discipline

A long collectibles guide is good when you need route explanation. It is less good when you just want to know what is left in Terra, whether you already cleaned Central Port, or if your Mini Cabin sweep is lagging behind your main 184 progress.

That is why this page keeps the tracking surface narrow: verified sector totals, separate Mini Cabin coverage, exportable saves, and one notes field for route friction that public checklists usually ignore.

Good fit: players doing a full first run, post-Lim-Eraser return pass, or Unknown Signal cleanup.

// Known Limits

What This Page Does Not Pretend To Do

This is not a replacement for every per-item collectible route. Public guide coverage still varies in how it labels certain item positions, especially when one page folds Red Zone rewards, side rewards, or return-only pickups into a different naming scheme.

Practical choice: use this page to track totals cleanly, then jump to the relevant sector or collectible guide for exact spot-by-spot routing.

// FAQ

Checklist Tool FAQ

How many collectibles does this tool track? +
The main checklist tracks 184 standard sector collectibles across the five main sectors. Mini Cabins are separated into their own 15-slot panel because current guide coverage treats them as a distinct collectible set.
Does this replace the longer collectibles guide? +
Not really. This page is the cleaner tracking surface, while the parent collectibles pages are better for exact routes, side-system explanation, and type-specific context.
Can I clean up missed collectibles after the story? +
Current walkthrough and completion coverage consistently points to Unknown Signal Mode as the safest cleanup window for missed sector items, which is why this page is still useful after the ending.
Why separate Mini Cabins from the main progress bar? +
Because they behave differently in player routing. Current coverage treats the full set as 15 total, three per sector, and they are commonly discussed outside the normal sector-completion math.
