// What They Do

Why Safe Boxes Matter

Upgrade Fuel

Broad guide coverage treats Safe Boxes as one of the most reliable ways to collect upgrade-driving loot, especially Lunafilament and other high-value optional rewards.

Route Pressure

Some boxes are trivial; others are effectively route puzzles hidden behind `Holo-Walls`, branch choices, or story-locked crystals. That is why Safe Boxes are often the collectible type most likely to create return trips.

100% Relevance

If you care about clean sector completion, Safe Boxes are too numerous to ignore. They are one of the main reasons players miss full completion on a first sweep.

// Backtracking

When To Come Back Later

`Lim Eraser` Cleanup

The first major Safe Box backtracking wave happens after you learn how to break blue crystal barriers with Lim Eraser. Reliable sector coverage and blue-crystal guides point back to missed paths in Solar Power Plant and Mass Production Array.

Safe rule: if a box is behind blue filament in Sectors 1–2, tag it for your post-Sector-2 return instead of overrouting your first pass.

`Cleansing` Cleanup

The later cleanup wave centers on Cleansing. High-confidence coverage specifically calls out late-access Safe Boxes in Terra Dome and Lunum Mines, with Central Port progression acting as the trigger point for that return route.

Known examples: Terra Dome `Safe Box 8` and `Safe Box 9`, plus Lunum Mines `Safe Box 2`, `Safe Box 3`, and `Safe Box 13`, are all repeatedly identified as `Cleansing`-locked.

`Unknown Signal Mode` Cleanup

The safest final pass is still Unknown Signal Mode. Multiple collectible guides agree that nothing is permanently missable, and that post-story cleanup keeps your collected items while exposing the remaining gaps sector by sector.

Best use case: if you only care about a clean 100% file, this is the least stressful moment to finish leftover boxes.

// Best Route

Safest Full-Clear Route

First Campaign Sweep

  1. Collect obvious on-path Safe Boxes in every sector.
  2. Do not overforce blue or red filament routes on first sight.
  3. After `Lim Eraser`, revisit Sectors 1–2 only for the clearly unlocked blue-filament boxes.
  4. After `Cleansing`, decide whether to do immediate cleanup or save it for post-story.

Completionist Cleanup

If your goal is zero wasted movement, the cleaner route is often: main story first, then `Unknown Signal Mode`, then a sector-by-sector Safe Box pass starting from the earliest backtrack-heavy areas and ending in Central Port.

Main mistake: turning every suspicious wall into a detour on your first visit. Pragmata is much friendlier if you trust the cleanup loop.

// FAQ

Safe Boxes FAQ

How many Safe Boxes are in Pragmata? +

Current sector-by-sector collectible coverage supports a total of 70 Safe Boxes across the full game: 3 in Solar Power Plant, 17 in Mass Production Array, 20 in Terra Dome, 17 in Lunum Mines, and 13 in Central Port.

Can I permanently miss Safe Boxes in Pragmata? +

No. Current collectible guides agree that nothing is permanently missable because Unknown Signal Mode lets you revisit sectors and finish cleanup after the story.

Which abilities matter most for Safe Box cleanup? +

The two main cleanup abilities are Lim Eraser for blue filament paths and Cleansing for later red-filament or dead-filament routes.

Which sector has the most Safe Boxes? +

Terra Dome has the highest confirmed Safe Box count, with 20 in total.
