// What They Do

More Than Lore

REMs are story-heavy collectibles, but they also feed progression. Current guide coverage agrees you earn a Cabin Coin by taking a REM back to the Shelter and gifting it to Diana, which ties them directly into Cabin's Stamp Club.

// Distribution

Where They Cluster

The opening sector is light and mostly tutorial-like, but Sector 2 and Terra Dome each carry four confirmed REMs. If your Cabin Coin total feels low midway through the game, these are the first places worth auditing.

// Safety

Route-Safe Cleanup

Current major collectible guides treat REMs the same way as the rest of the checklist: nothing is permanently missable, and post-story cleanup remains the safest answer if you only care about a clean file.

// Shelter Value

Why REMs Matter

Cabin Coins

1 Coin Per Gift

Current guide coverage agrees the Cabin Coin payout happens after you return to Shelter and gift the REM to Diana. Simply picking up a REM in the field is not the same as converting it into Stamp Club progress.

Shelter Loop

Back at Base

REMs fit naturally into the between-mission rhythm: collect them during a sector pass, then cash them in when you return to the Shelter and continue spending Cabin Coins on Stamp Boards.

Completion

Tracked Progress

REMs are not a side novelty like Mini Cabins. They are part of the tracked collectible mix used by major guides when discussing 100 percent sector cleanup.

// Best Route

Safest REM Cleanup Route

Early Campaign Sweep

  1. Always bank the obvious REMs in Solar Power Plant before you leave the sector.
  2. In Mass Production Array, slow down whenever a REM is visible behind fencing or glass and note the route origin before pushing the objective.
  3. Do not leave a sector with unspent REM gifts for too long if you need more Cabin Coins for Stamp Boards.

Midgame Audit

The strongest audit checkpoint is after Sector 3. By then, you should have cleared all of Solar Power Plant, all of Mass Production Array, and the full Terra Dome REM set if you were sweeping carefully. If your total is badly off there, backtracking later becomes less predictable.

Most common drift: leaving behind a visible REM in Sector 2 or Terra Dome and assuming the collectible log will make the path obvious later.

Unknown Signal Finish

If your goal is a clean file rather than a first-pass collectible run, current coverage still points to Unknown Signal Mode as the safest cleanup window. Multiple guides treat REMs as non-missable and compatible with post-story revisits.

Best use case: finish the campaign, reopen the map, then sweep Sector 2 through Sector 5 in order while checking your remaining REM gaps.

// FAQ

REM FAQ

How many REMs are in Pragmata? +

Current sector-by-sector collectible coverage supports a total of 16 Read Earth Memories across the full game.

What do REMs do in Pragmata? +

REMs are Read Earth Memories that you bring back to the Shelter and gift to Diana. Current guide coverage agrees each gift awards 1 Cabin Coin for Stamp Club progression.

Which sectors have the most REMs? +

Mass Production Array and Terra Dome are tied for the highest confirmed REM count, with 4 each.

Can I permanently miss REMs in Pragmata? +

Current major collectible guides treat REMs as non-missable because post-story cleanup in Unknown Signal Mode allows you to revisit sectors and recover leftovers.
