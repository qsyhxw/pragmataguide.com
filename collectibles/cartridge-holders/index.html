Pragmata Cartridge Holder Guide

Cartridge Holders are one of the cleanest survivability upgrades in Pragmata. This page tracks the 6 confirmed holders, separates the 2 field pickups from the 4 Cabin's Stamp Club rewards, and gives you a low-friction route to max out Repair Cartridge space without over-backtracking.

// Function

What Cartridge Holders Do

Healing Capacity

More Repair Cartridges

Current guide coverage agrees that each Cartridge Holder adds 1 extra Repair Cartridge slot. If you complete the full set, your carrying capacity grows six times.

Boss Prep

High Early Value

The first Solar pickup lands early enough to matter for the opening combat stretch, and the second field pickup shows up before the route fully widens out. That makes Cartridge Holders one of the best low-effort durability upgrades on a blind run.

Progression Split

Sector + Shelter Loop

This collectible type is unusual because only 2 are found in sectors. The other 4 come from Cabin's Stamp Club, so a full clear depends on both map cleanup and Shelter progression.

// Upgrade Value

Why They Matter

They Solve Attrition

A single extra Repair Cartridge slot may not sound dramatic on paper, but it smooths out the long combat rooms where you take chip damage, hesitate to heal, and then arrive at the next encounter under pressure.

They Stack Cleanly

Unlike build choices that ask you to commit to one weapon or one hacking style, Cartridge Holders help almost every run. They pair especially well with the broader upgrade planning on the Upgrades guide and with the longer cleanup route on the Storage Expanders page.

They Reward Shelter Follow-Through

Four of the six holders are tied to Shelter reward boards, so this page naturally overlaps with REM gifting, Cabin Coin planning, and the Pragmata all collectibles guide if you want to place Cartridge Holders inside the wider 100% route instead of treating them as a standalone upgrade checklist.

// Best Route

Safest Cleanup Route

1. Take The Two Field Pickups Early

  1. Grab the Solar Power Plant holder in The Concourse as part of your normal Sector 1 path.
  2. Sweep the Mass Production Array Business District pickup while Sector 2 is still fresh in your head.
  3. That leaves the rest of the set in Shelter instead of scattered across late sectors.

2. Audit Stamp Club Between Sectors

Because four holders live in Cabin's Stamp Club, the cleanest habit is to check the Shelter reward boards every time you finish a major sector loop. That keeps the Trainee and Associate rewards from quietly drifting into your postgame backlog.

Common miss pattern: players remember the two map pickups, then forget that Specialist and Director each still hide another holder.

3. Use Post-Story Cleanup For The Final Audit

If your file is still short, current guide coverage points to post-story cleanup and Unknown Signal as the safest window to reconcile leftovers. At that point, your checklist is simple: confirm both field pickups, then recheck all four Stamp Club boards.

Fastest audit order: Solar holder, Sector 2 holder, Trainee board, Associate board, Specialist board, Director board.

// FAQ

Cartridge Holder FAQ

How many Cartridge Holders are in Pragmata? +

Current broad collectible coverage supports a total of 6 Cartridge Holders: 2 in the field and 4 in Cabin's Stamp Club.

What does a Cartridge Holder do? +

Each Cartridge Holder adds 1 Repair Cartridge slot, which increases Hugh's healing capacity.

Where are the two field Cartridge Holders? +

Current sector guide coverage places one in Solar Power Plant's Concourse area and one in Mass Production Array's Business District near the Office Space Escape Hatch route.

Which Stamp Club boards award Cartridge Holders? +

Current reward coverage lists one Cartridge Holder each on the Trainee, Associate, Specialist, and Director Cabin's Stamp Club boards.
