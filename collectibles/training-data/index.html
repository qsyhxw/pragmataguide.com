// What They Do

Sim Unlocks

Training Data is not a currency spend. Its main value is unlocking additional Training Simulations at the Shelter, which is why these pickups matter even when they do not look flashy in the field.

// Distribution

Late-Sector Spread

All six confirmed Training Data pickups sit in the back half of the campaign. Terra Dome starts the chain, Lunum Mines continues it, and Central Port finishes it, which makes this collectible type naturally bridge into endgame and cleanup planning.

// Safety

Route-Safe Cleanup

Current major collectible guides treat Training Data as non-missable checklist items. If you are not chasing every simulation unlock on the first pass, Unknown Signal cleanup remains the safest answer.

// Bridge Value

Why Training Data Matters

Upgrades

Shelter Progression Loop

Training Data does not directly buy nodes or firmware upgrades, but it expands the Training Simulation side loop that feeds more rewards back into your broader Shelter progression.

Trophies

Master of the Simulator

The strongest trophy bridge is obvious: full Training Sim completion leads into Master of the Simulator, and Training Data is one of the systems that opens more of those sims.

Post-Game

Unknown Signal and NG+

Current VGC post-game coverage treats Training Data as a one-time collectible that stays collected in New Game+, which makes Unknown Signal cleanup the safer place to finish any leftovers.

// Best Route

Safest Training Data Cleanup Route

Early Campaign Sweep

  1. Do not waste time searching Sector 1 or Sector 2 for Training Data because current full coverage lists none there.
  2. When you reach Terra Dome, treat it as the first real Training Data audit point and try to leave Sector 3 with both pickups.
  3. If you are also routing upgrades, fold Terra's two Training Data pickups into the same sweep as your early Shield and firmware planning.

Midgame Audit

The cleanest audit checkpoint is after Lunum Mines. By then, a careful collectible route should have all four Training Data pickups from Sector 3 and Sector 4. If you are still below four there, your Shelter sim unlock pacing will be behind for the rest of the run.

Most common drift: grabbing one of the two Terra or one of the two Lunum pickups and assuming the missing pair will be obvious later.

Unknown Signal Finish

If your goal is a clean file rather than a first-pass collectible run, current coverage still points to Unknown Signal Mode as the safest cleanup window. Full collectible coverage treats Training Data as non-missable, and post-game coverage treats it as a one-time collectible that will already be collected once you move into New Game+.

Best use case: finish the campaign, reopen the map, then sweep Terra Dome, Lunum Mines, and Central Port in that order while checking the remaining total against 6.

// FAQ

Training Data FAQ

How many Training Data collectibles are in Pragmata? +

Current full collectible coverage supports a total of 6 Training Data collectibles across the full game.

What does Training Data do in Pragmata? +

Training Data unlocks additional Training Simulation content at the Shelter. It matters most if you are pushing deeper into sim rewards or the Master of the Simulator trophy path.

Which sectors have the most Training Data? +

Terra Dome, Lunum Mines, and Central Port are tied for the highest confirmed count, with 2 Training Data pickups each.

Can I permanently miss Training Data in Pragmata? +

Current major collectible guides treat Training Data as non-missable because post-story cleanup in Unknown Signal Mode allows you to revisit sectors and recover leftovers.
