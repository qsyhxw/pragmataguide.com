// Sector 3 Boss

Pragmata Garden Keeper Boss Guide

`Garden Keeper` is the fight where simple pattern recognition stops being enough. Compared with the earlier bosses, it moves faster, punishes early dodges harder, and makes weak-point priority matter much more. If you treat it as a timing fight first and a damage race second, the encounter becomes a lot more readable.

Fight Plan Best Prep Sector 3 Walkthrough

Quick Facts

  • Location: `Terrarium`, at the end of `Terra Dome`.
  • Role: third major boss and the clearest dodge-timing check so far.
  • Weak point priority: tail first, underside second, head last.
  • Best common tools: `Code Generator`, `Sticky Bombs`, and `Overdrive Protocol`.
Interpretation note: source coverage is consistent on the weak-point order and late-phase pressure, but less precise about exact attack names and timing windows. This page keeps the wording practical and avoids hard frame-level claims.

Weak Point

Tail

Current launch-week coverage is highly consistent that the tail is the top-value target.

Late Dodges

Code Generator

// Recommended Prep

Best First-Clear Setup

Bring `Code Generator`

Launch-week guide coverage repeatedly highlights `Code Generator` because the boss gets harder to manage once hacking pressure increases and its later-phase movement opens larger matrices.

Use `Sticky Bombs` For Moving Openings

Because Garden Keeper rarely sits still for long, sticky burst tools are more forgiving than weapons that demand perfect stand-and-fire windows.

Save `Overdrive Protocol` For Safe Tail Windows

The tail is the best target, but not always the safest to chase. When Overdrive or another clean opening pins the fight down, that is the moment to convert your strongest damage.

// Boss Plan

How To Beat Garden Keeper

1. Treat It As A Timing Fight First

The biggest early mistake is reacting too soon. Garden Keeper closes distance quickly, chains pressure, and punishes panic movement. Your first job is not maximizing DPS. It is learning when not to dodge yet.

2. Follow The Tail-First Damage Rule

Cross-source coverage is most stable on one damage rule:

Tail first, underside second, head last: this is the most consistent weak-point priority in public coverage. If the tail is accessible, it is usually the best place to cash out. If not, the underside is still better than settling for low-value head damage.

3. Wait For Planted Tail Openings

Several guides single out the planted-tail moments as the cleanest burst opportunities, especially when the tail stays in the ground after a committed attack. Those windows matter because they let you hit the highest value weak point without gambling on a wild chase.

4. Expect A Meaner Late Phase Below Half Health

Public launch-week coverage is broadly aligned that the fight becomes nastier below roughly half health, with wall-clinging pressure, lightning-style threats, and aerial tail attacks entering the conversation. You do not need the exact phase script memorized, but you do need to know the second half is less forgiving.

5. Use Hacking Tools To Keep The Fight Manageable

Code-support and sticky burst tools appear often in Garden Keeper coverage for a reason: the fight becomes more stable when you reduce how much perfect tracking it asks of you. If your setup helps turn a brief dodge success into real damage, it is probably worth more than greedier options.

The cleanest practical rule is simple: survive the chain, then punish the tail. Garden Keeper rewards calm timing more than flashy aggression.

// After The Fight

What Garden Keeper Unlocks

The most stable reward claim across sources is that defeating `Garden Keeper` gives you Shelter License Key #3. That advances your hub to the next major progression step after Terra Dome.

Public upgrade coverage also links the post-Terra-Dome Shelter tier to more build depth, including additional Training Simulations, the Specialist Stamp Board, and stronger offensive or defensive options.

// FAQ

Garden Keeper FAQ

Where do you fight Garden Keeper in Pragmata? +

Garden Keeper is the mandatory main story boss in the Terrarium at the end of the Terra Dome sector.

What is Garden Keeper's weak point priority? +

The safest current answer is tail first, underside second, and head last. That ordering is the most consistent damage-priority guidance in public boss coverage.

Why do players struggle with this fight? +

Most early failures come from dodging too soon, chasing bad damage angles, or missing the transition into the boss's harsher late phase.

What tools are commonly recommended? +

Code Generator, Sticky Bombs, and careful use of Overdrive Protocol are common public recommendations because they help convert short openings into safer damage.

What do you unlock after beating Garden Keeper? +

The high-confidence reward is Shelter License Key #3, which advances Shelter progression after Terra Dome.
