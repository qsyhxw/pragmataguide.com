// After Credits

Pragmata Post-Game Guide

Once the credits roll, Pragmata opens into two different follow-up routes: Unknown Signal, which acts as the game's real cleanup-and-challenge layer, and New Game Plus, which lets you replay the campaign with your build intact. The safest broad advice from current guide coverage is simple: do Unknown Signal first, then treat NG+ as your replay or build-maxing route.

Unknown Signal NG+ Best Order FAQ

Quick Facts

  • Unlock: both modes appear after one full story clear.
  • Unknown Signal: returns you to a completed save near the final-boss stretch.
  • Hidden Chamber: this is where the post-game missions live.
  • Best use of NG+: replay, build carryover, and optional extra challenge.
Planning tip: current coverage is much more consistent about what Unknown Signal does than about every exact NG+ ruleset. This page keeps the NG+ section honest and a little more conservative where source wording diverges.

Unknown Signal Explained

How It Starts

Stronger launch-week coverage agrees on the basic flow: after you finish the main story once, the main menu gains an Unknown Signal option tied to a completed save file.

When you enter it, the game places you back at the Shelter near the end of the story route, then opens access to the Hidden Chamber.

What It Is Actually For

The highest-confidence description is not “bonus mode” but post-story cleanup and challenge route. This is where completionist coverage consistently places sector return trips, late rewards, Hidden Chamber missions, and the broader true-ending chain.

What is safe to say about rewards: current sources consistently treat Unknown Signal as the source of meaningful post-game rewards such as strong Mods, outfits, resources, and late build options, even when individual reward lists vary by guide.

// Replay Route

New Game Plus Explained

What NG+ Does

High-confidence coverage agrees on the core idea: NG+ restarts the main story while preserving the important parts of your build and progression so a second campaign run feels far more flexible.

What Usually Carries Over

The stable overlap across guides is that your major gear, unlocks, and upgrade progress carry into NG+. That is the part broad enough to rely on safely.

What To Use It For

Treat NG+ as the place to replay the campaign with a mature loadout, test stronger weapon combinations, and enjoy a less resource-starved route after your first clear.

NG+ caution: launch-week guides do not describe every NG+ rule the same way, especially around exact difficulty pairing and a few reset details. This page intentionally avoids locking those disputed specifics into hard facts.

// FAQ

Post-Game FAQ

What unlocks after finishing Pragmata once? +

Current launch-week guide coverage agrees that finishing the main story unlocks both Unknown Signal and New Game Plus, giving you one post-story cleanup route and one replay route.

Should you play Unknown Signal or NG+ first? +

The safest broad recommendation is Unknown Signal first, because stronger current guides treat it as Pragmata's real post-game completion path and the route tied to the extra ending payoff.

What is the Hidden Chamber? +

The Hidden Chamber is the post-game area opened through Unknown Signal. Current coverage consistently describes it as the hub for the mode's extra challenge missions or pods.

Does NG+ replace Unknown Signal? +

No. Current guide coverage treats them as different routes: Unknown Signal is the structured post-game challenge and cleanup mode, while NG+ is the replay mode with progression carryover.
