Architect's Vault Rewards

The most stable final reward list is the one repeated by GamesRadar+ and echoed by follow-up Black Box coverage: Lim Cannon, Black Box Mod, and Obsidia outfits for Hugh and Diana.

`Lim Cannon` is the gameplay reward, `Black Box Mod` is the narrative-critical reward, and the `Obsidia` outfits are the cosmetic reward layer. That makes the vault a clean blend of build, style, and ending progression.

Best Practical Read

  • The first Hidden Chamber mission matters immediately because it is the clearest early route to the late `Shelter License Key Level 5` upgrade.
  • The `100% sector` missions are not just ending chores. They also feed the broader reward pool, so reward-focused players should not put them off forever.
  • Once all 10 are done, the Architect's vault becomes the real finish line for rewards.
  • If your goal is the extra ending, treat the `Black Box Mod` as the capstone reward, not just another item in the pile.

// Best Route

Safest Reward Route

1. Clear Alpha Early

The first Hidden Chamber clear is the safest early target because it is the cleanest documented route to `Shelter License Key Level 5`.

2. Alternate Bosses and 100%

Rotate between Enhanced bosses and sector completion so you keep unlocking pods instead of grinding one side of the system too hard.

3. Treat the Vault as Phase Two

`Lim Cannon`, `Black Box`, and the outfits are your final reward layer, so plan around the full 10-mission clear rather than expecting them piecemeal.

4. Finish with Black Box Equipped

If you care about story payoff, do not forget the final equip step. The `Black Box Mod` only matters when it is active for the final boss rematch.

Most common mix-up: players often bundle `Sim Pod completion`, `vault rewards`, and `true ending trigger` into one step. They are three linked steps: clear missions, open the vault, then equip `Black Box` and rematch the final boss.
