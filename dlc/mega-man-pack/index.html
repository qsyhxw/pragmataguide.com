PRAGMATA Mega Man Pack

The Mega Man Pack is a free cosmetic and music add-on for PRAGMATA. It requires the base game and is listed for Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2.

Free DLCBase game requiredAll launch platforms
Quick answer: claim the free add-on from your platform's official store, using the same platform account as your PRAGMATA base game. Official descriptions list two outfit sets for both Hugh and Diana, gestures, and BGM that can be changed from the shelter. They do not advertise a new story mission, weapon, or combat ability.

// Verified contents

What the pack includes

Outfits

Two sets for both characters

The Nintendo and Steam descriptions say the pack adds two outfit sets for both Hugh and Diana. The official artwork shows Mega Man-themed looks for the pair.

Extras

Gestures and shelter BGM

The same official listings include gestures and BGM. Nintendo specifically says the BGM can be changed from the shelter.

Scope check: the official product descriptions do not list a campaign chapter, quest, weapon, stat bonus, or new ability. Treat this as a cosmetic/music pack unless Capcom publishes additional contents.

// Claim and verify

How to install it

  1. Open the matching official listing. Use the store for the platform where you own PRAGMATA.
  2. Claim the free add-on. Sign in with the account that owns or can access the base game, then choose the store's Get, Add, or Download action.
  3. Verify ownership and installation. In your platform's game or add-on management screen, confirm that the Mega Man Pack appears as owned and installed.
  4. Relaunch PRAGMATA. If the content does not appear, fully close the game, confirm it is updated, then restart it. The official descriptions do not specify a separate quest or exact unlock trigger.

// If it is missing

Safe checks before troubleshooting

  • Confirm you claimed the add-on for the same platform and account that owns the base game.
  • Check that the store page shows the pack as owned, then review the platform's installed add-ons list.
  • Install any pending PRAGMATA update and restart the game.
  • If the official store says the product is unavailable, check the account/store region. Availability and displayed dates can vary by region.

This page does not claim an unverified in-game menu path. The only menu detail in current official descriptions is that the included BGM can be changed from the shelter.
