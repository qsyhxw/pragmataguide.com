// Read First

Before You Enter

Sector 2 is significantly larger than Sector 1 and introduces several new enemy types: the fast-rolling Spider and the shield-generating Executor. The main goal is to hack 6 beacons scattered across the sector to unlock the Comms Tower gate — you can do them in any order.

Shelter prep · Use your Shelter License Key from Sector 1 first. Expand your Firmware Updater slots and print the Shockwave Gun if you haven't already. Upgrade Diana's Hacking before deploying — Executors are far more dangerous than Walkers.

Executor mechanic · When an Executor's health gets low, four antennas grow from its face (like a Demogorgon) that block Diana's hacking entirely. Destroy the antennas with Hugh's gun first, then resume hacking normally.

Nothing missable · All 39 collectibles remain available in Unknown Signal mode after completing the story. Items behind Lim Eraser blockades are flagged — get the Eraser in Block 5 and immediately backtrack.

B1

// Block 1

Test Site Entrance

1

Arriving by tram, you see an intro cutscene of Diana chasing a holographic cat to the ledge. After it ends, move forward through the corridor. Check all side passages for Upgrade Components — several are hidden around corners in this zone.

2

IDUS immediately locks down the New York square. Beacon 1 is right in front of you — hack it with Diana for a quick warm-up. After hacking, Diana's Scan highlights a nearby hackable door. Go through it — you'll be chased by an Executor (don't engage yet, just run) until you reach the Shopping District Entrance Station Escape Hatch.

3

Encounter the Spider enemy here — a fast-rolling bot that charges straight at you. Don't let it close in. Hack it quickly to expose the core, then a single Grip Gun shot finishes it.

Backtrack items here: Safe Box 1 (diner, left side) and Safe Box 2 (Nouvelle Shop, right side) are both behind Lunafilament blockades. Lim Eraser required — unlock in Block 5 then return
B2

// Block 2 · 10 Collectibles

Shopping District

1

Activate the Escape Hatch as you enter, then jump over the truck blocking the path. Ahead is your first Red Zone door — you'll need a Red Gate Key. Find one in the room with the "Resident Devil III" poster on the wall. Inside that same room is also the Multihack Hacking Node — links damage between multiple enemies simultaneously.

2

Through the open door under the "Orbit Orbit" sign, pick up the Riot Blaster. Grabbing it triggers a lockdown — multiple waves of enemies including an Executor spawn. Clear them, then hack Beacon 2.

3

Hack the control panel to create moving platforms, then cross the bridge to the Interconnecting Passage Station Escape Hatch. This Escape Hatch is the boundary between Shopping District and Side Alley — activate it before proceeding.

Mini Cabin 1: In the Shopping District, after passing the first Escape Hatch. When you reach the mannequin window with the "Orbit Our World" neon sign, look up and glide to the right side — the figure is on the roof. Listen for the "ta-da" sound.
Lim Eraser backtrack: Safe Box 5 in this block is behind a Lunafilament barrier. Return after Block 5
B3

// Block 3 · 4 Collectibles

Side Alley

1

Drop from the building to the street below. Beside the white door, pick up the Charge Piercer — the best weapon for the upcoming Creator boss. It fires a slow but powerful piercing laser that passes through multiple enemies in a line. Always charge it fully before firing at exposed weak points.

2

Go through the white door — your first full Executor fight (not a chase). Use the Charge Piercer on its chest and cooling rods on the back. When low-HP antennas appear and block hacking: shoot off the antennas first, then resume Diana's hack.

3

After clearing the Executor, hack the wall terminal to raise platforms. Hack Beacon 3 on the structure ahead. Open the blue chest for the Relay Amplifier Mod — increases Diana's hacking range.

B4

// Block 4 · 10 Collectibles

Business District

1

This block introduces the Lim Recycler environmental hazard — a large compactor you can use against enemy groups. A nearby laptop explains it. Pull enemies under a recycler with the Decoy Generator and then hack the terminal to destroy them all at once.

2

Hack Beacons 4 and 5 in this block. Beacon 4's enemy gauntlet is the hardest so far — waves of Watchers, Walkers, and an Executor. Use Overdrive Protocol (L3+R3) to knock several enemies down simultaneously.

3

After Beacon 4, look over the ledge near the pile of boxes — Mini Cabin 2 is on a platform against the wall below, almost hidden by decorative trees. Shoot it from the ledge edge.

4

Find the Decoy Generator in this block — available from the floor pickups in the Lim Recycler area. This defensive weapon creates a holographic Hugh to redirect enemy attention while you hack safely. Print it at the Unit Printer after this sector.

Round 'Em Up trophy: In Block 4 you'll enter a room with 6 yellow floor plates. When 3+ enemies are standing on them, hack the device on the left wall to activate the incinerator and destroy them all at once. This is the easiest window to earn this achievement.
Backtrack item: Safe Box 14 in this block is behind a Lunafilament barrier. Lim Eraser — Block 5 backtrack
B5

// Block 5 · 4 Collectibles · ★ Lim Eraser Unlocks Here

Lim Recycling Facility

Key Event — Lim Eraser

Diana acquires the Lim Eraser from a terminal near the end of this facility. This unlocks the ability to destroy blue Lunafilament barriers — the walls that have blocked several collectibles across Sector 1 and 2. This is an automatic story event — no resources needed.

1

Enter the facility. Use the Decoy Generator to pull enemies under the Recycler conveyor belts, then hack the terminal to wipe them out — the most efficient method for this block's combat encounters.

2

Reach the terminal/pedestal room. Diana acquires the Lim Eraser — the game immediately lets you test it on nearby blue barriers. Use it on the barrier in the lab room to open the path forward.

3

The large Lim Recycler room ahead is packed with Walkers, Crushers, and an Executor. Clear them via the Recycler, then hack Beacon 6 — the final one. The Comms Tower gate opens.

4

Mini Cabin 3 is in the same room as Escape Hatch 4 — look in an open container near the door. Shoot it.

Do this now before continuing: With the Lim Eraser active, immediately fast-travel back to Sector 1 via the Shelter tram. Collect Storage Expander 1 (Generator Entrance) and Safe Box 2 + Pure Lunum 1 (Power Distribution Center) — all three were blocked on your first visit. Then return here for Block 6. This takes ~5 minutes and prevents a separate backtrack trip later.

B6

// Block 6

Comms Tower

1

The final Escape Hatch of the sector (Escape Hatch 5 — Comms Tower) is behind the gate you just opened with all 6 beacons. Activate it.

2

Return to the Shelter now. Stock Repair Cartridges to maximum, equip the Charge Piercer and Decoy Generator in your loadout, and spend any remaining Upgrade Components on Diana's Hacking. This is the last safe upgrade window before the boss.

3

Head to the rooftop. A cutscene plays — the holographic cat (IDUS's avatar) reappears and summons the Creator. The boss fight begins immediately after the cutscene ends.

// End of Sector 2

Boss: Creator (shown on-screen as "Gigantic Bot")

The Creator dwarfs the SectorGuard. It towers over the entire rooftop arena and mixes slow, telegraphed attacks with one arena-clearing move that can instantly drop you to 1 HP. Understanding when to climb and when to shoot is the key to this fight.

Recommended Loadout

★Charge Piercer — primary burst on weak points; pierces through hacking jammers
▸Shockwave Gun — close-range backup when the Charge Piercer is recharging
▸Decoy Generator — redirect Creator's attention during hacking
▸Freeze Hacking Node — locks Creator in place for clean shots on underside

Pick up extra Charge Piercer and Shockwave Gun ammo from the arena floor before triggering the fight.

Attack Patterns & Phase Guide

Core Attacks — All Phases
▸Hand Swipe / Arm Slam: Dodge into the swipe, not away — the invulnerability frames during the dodge carry Hugh through the attack. Dodging away often leaves you in the follow-up hitbox.
▸Stationary Neck Laser: Similarly, dodge toward the laser beam rather than away.
▸Arena Charge: Avoid the limbs — the central body mass is walkable between the legs.
⚠ Critical — Red Arena Glow

When the entire arena glows red, the Creator is charging its most dangerous attack. If it connects, Hugh drops to 1 HP regardless of current health.

Action: Stop everything and climb the Lunafilament pillars immediately. The tops of the pillars also provide the best angle to hit the hacking jammers on top of the Creator — deal with both at the same time.

Hacking Jammers — Mid-Fight
▸The Creator progressively blocks hacking panels by activating glowing sections on its limbs (joints, head, later its back). When these appear, Diana can no longer hack the boss.
▸Destroy glowing limb sections with Hugh's guns immediately — this is not optional. Losing hack access removes your only reliable damage window.
▸Use the pillar tops during the red arena phase to target head-mounted jammers from above.
Damage Loop & Weak Points
1Use Diana to hack — exposes the Creator's face (primary target) and optionally the underside belly (higher damage, awkward angle).
2Use the Freeze Hacking Node to lock the Creator in place — gives clean time to hit the underside weak point with a charged Charge Piercer shot.
3Later phases add a Lunafilament wave sweeping from the back of the arena — dash to the opposite end immediately when the animation begins.
Final Phase — Timed Hacking Sequence

When the Creator's health is fully depleted, the game locks into a timed hacking sequence. Navigate to the Open node before the timer expires — failure means death and restarting from the phase. Read the entire node layout before moving. Do not rush.

Reward: Defeating the Creator drops Shelter License Key #2. Return to the Shelter to unlock Tier 2 upgrades — expanded Firmware Updater capacity, new Unit Printer blueprints, and the Associate Board at Cabin's Stamp Club. A new sector (Terra Dome) is also unlocked on the tram map.

// Questions

Mass Production Array FAQ

How many collectibles are in the Mass Production Array? +

The Mass Production Array contains 39 collectibles across 6 blocks. Several Safe Boxes require the Lim Eraser (unlocked in Block 5 of this same sector). Nothing is permanently missable — all items are available in Unknown Signal mode post-story.

Where do I get the Charge Piercer? +

The Charge Piercer is on the ground next to the white door as you drop down into the Side Alley block (Block 3). It fires a chargeable piercing round — charge fully before using it on exposed weak points. It's the best weapon for the Creator boss fight later in this sector.

How do I beat the Creator boss? +

Hack with Diana to expose the face (primary weak point) and underside belly (higher damage). Use the Charge Piercer for burst damage. When glowing limb joints block hacking panels, shoot them off immediately. The critical rule: when the arena glows red, climb the Lunafilament pillars — the attack drops Hugh to 1 HP if it connects. The fight ends with a timed hacking sequence to the Open node — read the layout before moving.

How do I deal with Executor enemies? +

Target the chest (primary weak point) and cooling rods on the back (more damage) with the Charge Piercer after hacking. When health gets low, four antennas grow from its face blocking all hacking — shoot the antennas off with Hugh's gun first, then resume Diana's hack normally. If a charging Executor hits a wall, it's briefly stunned — use that window.

When should I backtrack with the Lim Eraser? +

Immediately after unlocking it in Block 5. Fast-travel to Sector 1 first (Storage Expander 1 in Generator Entrance + Safe Box 2 + Pure Lunum 1 in Power Distribution Center), then sweep through the Sector 2 flagged items (Test Site SB1&2, Shopping District SB5, Business District SB14). This takes ~10 minutes and clears all Lim Eraser-gated backtracking at once.

What is the "Round 'Em Up" achievement and where do I get it? +

In Block 4 (Business District), you'll enter a room with 6 yellow floor plates. When more than 3 enemies are standing on the plates, hack the device on the left wall to activate an incinerator that destroys all of them at once. Use the Decoy Generator to lure enemies onto the plates if they won't stand there naturally.
