Pragmata Central Port Guide

Central Port is the final main sector in Pragmata and the last collectible-heavy zone before the endgame push. It is more compact than it first looks, but the route is dense: multiple Holo-Walls, several Dead Lunafilament cleanup points, and one of the clearest places in the game to lose time if you rush the critical path without checking side rooms.

Quick Facts

  • Area order: Port Entrance, Research Sector, Regolith Lab, Lunafilament Lab, Orbital Elevator.
  • Collectible total: 38 confirmed pickups for sector completion.
  • Key progression: Central Port is where Cleansing becomes part of the collectible route.
  • Cleanup: current launch-week guides agree nothing here is permanently missable.
Final-sector caution: this area sits right next to the game's end sequence, so it is worth clearing side rooms and Red Zones before committing to the last push.

// Verified Route

Central Port Walkthrough Structure

1. Port Entrance

Port Entrance is short but easy to under-loot because it front-loads a new mechanic. Cross-checked guides agree this opening block contains Escape Hatch 1, REM 1 (Parasol), Mod 1 (Untapped Potential), and Mini Cabin 1. It also serves as the first strong reminder that the sector expects you to look back and up, not only forward.

Before leaving the opening cargo area, do a last rear-facing sweep. Both the REM and the Mini Cabin are placed in ways that punish tunnel vision.

2. Research Sector

Research Sector is a compact setup block, but it quietly carries multiple important resources at once. Reliable guide coverage places Escape Hatch 2, Training Data 1, and a Red Zone Key in the first room after the long elevator view. Two Safe Boxes follow in the ruined outdoor section.

This is one of the better places in the game to pause and fully scan the room before moving, because the sector gets more maze-like immediately afterward.

3. Regolith Lab

Regolith Lab is the highest-density branch in the sector. It consistently appears as the block containing Escape Hatch 3, Mod 2 (Heat Transfer), Mod 3 (Cursed), Mini Cabin 2, REM 2 (Water Gun), and Pure Lunum 1–3, with two of those Pure Lunum rewards tied to the local Red Zone.

The easy miss here is the Holo-Wall after the Red Zone. If you do not habitually check the walls in that room, REM 2 is one of the simplest endgame misses to create for yourself.

4. Lunafilament Lab

Lunafilament Lab is the longest route block in Central Port and the one most dependent on methodical room clearing. Sources agree it contains Escape Hatch 4, the Lunafilament Lab Red Zone, Storage Expander 1, Pure Lunum 4–6, REM 3 (Sandcastle), Training Data 2, and the largest Safe Box concentration in the sector.

The two places most often flagged by guides are the 5-lock door sequence and the Dead Lunafilament-clearing side rooms. If you push the story line too hard here, you will almost always create a cleanup pass.

5. Orbital Elevator

Orbital Elevator closes the sector with a tighter checklist: Escape Hatch 5, Safe Box 9, Mini Cabin 3, a Holo-Wall branch containing Safe Box 10 and Safe Box 11, Mod 4 (Adrenaline Flood), Safe Box 12, Escape Hatch 6, and Safe Box 13.

The safest habit here is to take the lower side door before committing upward whenever the route forks. That catches the ledge-based mod path while the local geometry is still fresh in your head.

// Progression Note

Cleansing And Backtracking

Central Port is the sector where guide coverage consistently anchors the game's late cleanup loop. By this point you already have Lim Eraser and Deletion Protocol, and during Central Port you gain access to Cleansing, which turns several previously blocked routes into valid backtracking targets.

The most consistently repeated cleanup list includes earlier blocked items in Mass Production Array, Terra Dome, and Lunum Mines. If you care about minimizing total revisit time, this is the moment to decide whether you want to backtrack immediately or simply finish the story and clean everything in Unknown Signal Mode.

For broader system help, the best internal companions to this page are the Hacking Guide, Collectibles Guide, and Endings Guide.

// First-Pass Priorities

What To Do Before The Finale Push

Clear The Red Zones

Regolith Lab and Lunafilament Lab both hide meaningful resources behind optional challenge rooms. Leaving them for later is valid, but it adds another navigation pass through the densest part of the sector.

Scan For Holo-Walls

Central Port uses hidden walls more aggressively than the sector's total count suggests. REM 2 and the Orbital Elevator Safe Box branch are the most notable examples.

Do The Last Side Sweep

Once Orbital Elevator starts feeling like a point-of-no-return stretch, treat that instinct seriously and finish any collectible checks you still care about before advancing.

// FAQ

Central Port FAQ

How many collectibles are in Central Port? +

Central Port has 38 confirmed collectibles: 13 Safe Boxes, 6 Escape Hatches, 6 Pure Lunum, 4 Mods, 3 Mini Cabins, 3 REM, 1 Storage Expander, and 2 Training Data items.

Can I miss anything permanently in Central Port? +

Current launch-week coverage agrees that nothing in Central Port is permanently missable. Missed pickups can be collected later through Unknown Signal Mode.

Which Central Port area is easiest to miss items in? +

Regolith Lab and Lunafilament Lab are the two most commonly flagged problem areas. Regolith Lab hides a key REM behind a Holo-Wall, while Lunafilament Lab combines Dead Lunafilament, multi-lock routing, and the biggest Safe Box cluster in the sector.

Why is Central Port important for backtracking? +

Because Central Port is where Cleansing becomes part of the route. Once you have it, several blocked collectible paths in older sectors open up, which makes this the natural pivot point for a full cleanup run.
