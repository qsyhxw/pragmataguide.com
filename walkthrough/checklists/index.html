// Cleanup Route

The Safest 100% Route Without Overrouting

  1. Play Solar Power Plant normally. Do not worry about forcing every blocked pickup on the first pass.
  2. Finish Mass Production Array and unlock Lim Eraser. This is the first meaningful backtrack moment in current walkthrough coverage.
  3. Return to Solar Power Plant first. It is the cleanest place to cash in the earliest Lim Eraser-gated items.
  4. Continue through Terra Dome and Lunum Mines. These are the denser middle sectors, so checklist discipline matters more than speed here.
  5. Treat Central Port as the last main-story setup phase. It is the most natural point to prepare for post-story cleanup.
  6. Use Unknown Signal for final sweep work. That remains the most broadly supported answer for missed collectible cleanup.
Important:
Not every route decision needs to be solved on first contact. Current guide coverage is much more consistent about the two cleanup windows than about shaving every revisit to zero.

// Key Gates

The Two Route Gates You Actually Need To Remember

Lim Eraser

Multiple walkthrough sources agree that Lim Eraser is a mandatory Sector 2 story unlock. For checklist planning, that makes it the first real “return now if you care about 100%” signal, especially for Solar Power Plant cleanup and a few delayed Sector 2 pickups.

Late-Game Red-Barrier Cleanup

Current guide coverage also agrees that a late-game barrier-clearing ability becomes part of the route by Central Port and matters for final cleanup in earlier areas. This page keeps that reminder broad on purpose, because source naming and exact timing are less consistently worded than the overall cleanup implication.

Best memory shortcut: one cleanup pass after Lim Eraser, one full cleanup pass after the story.

// FAQ

Checklist Hub FAQ

How many main sectors are in Pragmata? +

Pragmata's main story runs through five numbered sectors: Solar Power Plant, Mass Production Array, Terra Dome, Lunum Mines, and Central Port. Many guides also count the opening Prologue separately.

When should I start cleanup in Pragmata? +

The cleanest early cleanup point is right after unlocking the Lim Eraser in Sector 2, because it opens blocked pickups in the Solar Power Plant and parts of the Mass Production Array. Full endgame cleanup is safest after the story through Unknown Signal mode.

Can I miss collectibles permanently in Pragmata? +

Current walkthrough coverage consistently says no. Missed collectibles can be cleaned up later, and Unknown Signal mode is commonly cited as the safest post-story cleanup window.

Which sector has the biggest collectible load? +

Terra Dome is the largest confirmed sector by collectible count in current guide coverage, ahead of Lunum Mines, Mass Production Array, Central Port, and Solar Power Plant.
