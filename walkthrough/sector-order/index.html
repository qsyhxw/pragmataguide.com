// Main Story Sequence

Pragmata Chapter and Sector Order

  1. Prologue: the opening setup chapter before the numbered sector flow properly begins.
  2. Sector 1 - Solar Power Plant: the first full sector and your real tutorial stretch.
  3. Sector 2 - Mass Production Array: the first major route-expanding sector and the Lim Eraser unlock point.
  4. Sector 3 - Terra Dome: the largest early-midgame sector and a noticeable jump in collectible density.
  5. Sector 4 - Lunum Mines: the low-gravity lunar sector and the last big pre-endgame route block.
  6. Sector 5 - Central Port: the final main story sector before post-game content opens.
  7. Unknown Signal: post-story cleanup and challenge content, not part of the main five-sector story chain.
Shortest answer: if you searched for mission order, just play the five numbered sectors in order after the Prologue. There is no alternate main campaign route you need to worry about.
Best route nuance: although the sector order is fixed, the first smart cleanup pause is after the Lim Eraser unlock in Sector 2.
Do not overread Unknown Signal: it matters a lot for cleanup and endings, but it does not replace or reshuffle the main chapter order.

// Terminology

Chapter Order vs Sector Order

Most Pragmata pages use sector order and mission order almost interchangeably for the five numbered story zones. The one thing that makes the wording look different is the Prologue.

So the clean reading is: 6 chapters including Prologue, or 5 numbered main sectors once the real sector chain begins.

Easy memory rule: if someone says “chapter order,” include the Prologue. If someone says “sector order,” they usually mean Solar through Central.

// FAQ

Sector Order FAQ

What is the sector order in Pragmata? +
The fixed main order is Solar Power Plant, Mass Production Array, Terra Dome, Lunum Mines, and Central Port, after the opening Prologue.
How many chapters are in Pragmata? +
The cleanest walkthrough reading is six chapters if you include the Prologue, or five main sectors once you start counting only the numbered story zones.
Can I choose a different mission order? +
Not for the main campaign flow. The order of the numbered sectors is fixed, even though cleanup timing and post-story routing can branch later.
Where does Unknown Signal fit? +
Unknown Signal comes after Central Port as post-game content. It is important for cleanup and endings, but it is not a sixth numbered sector in the main story route.
