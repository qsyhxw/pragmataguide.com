Pragmata IDUS & Delphi Explained

If you keep seeing IDUS, Delphi, and the Cradle in Pragmata coverage, the safest high-level reading is simple: the Cradle is the moonbase, Delphi is the corporate force tied to the project, and IDUS is the rogue AI crisis that turns the station into a survival story.

// Setting

What The Cradle Is

The Main Lunar Base

The strongest official description of the Cradle is that it is the massive lunar base where Pragmata takes place. It is not a throwaway background term. It is the physical setting that holds the Shelter, the hostile machine network, and most of the game's mystery.

The Lunafilament Crisis Starts Here

Official setup material says communication is lost with a lunar station dedicated to the research and development of Lunafilament. That is the crisis point that pulls Hugh's team into the story before the quake turns everything worse.

Best safe shorthand:
When players ask “what is the Cradle?”, the most reliable short answer is “the Moon facility where Pragmata happens and where the Lunafilament disaster unfolds.”

// Corporate Layer

What Delphi Means

Public launch-week coverage consistently treats Delphi Corporation as the corporate force behind the larger moonbase project. GamesRadar's broader series coverage ties Delphi to third-generation Lunafilament and advanced generative AI, while review coverage frames the Cradle as a facility operated by or deeply connected to Delphi.

That means “Delphi” is not just a name-drop. It is the term that links the technology promise, the corporate ambition, and the setting-wide disaster.

Stable reading:
Even without forcing every hidden boardroom detail, current sources strongly support Delphi as the corporation most closely tied to Lunafilament, AI, and the Cradle project.

// AI Threat

What IDUS Is

1. A Rogue AI

Official launch messaging explicitly describes IDUS as the lunar research station's rogue AI.

2. The Hostile Force

Review coverage repeatedly presents IDUS as the hostile artificial intelligence Hugh and Diana are up against.

3. The Crisis Link

IDUS is the term that turns the moonbase mystery into an immediate survival problem.

The cleanest verified way to explain IDUS is as the rogue AI at the center of the station's collapse. Capcom's launch messaging points to “the mystery surrounding the lunar research station's rogue AI: IDUS,” and review coverage consistently treats it as the hostile intelligence driving the station-wide danger.

That is enough for a reliable story page. It tells readers why the facility is hostile, why Hugh and Diana are constantly under pressure, and why the setting terms keep circling back to AI control instead of just generic robot enemies.

// Connection Map

How These Terms Fit Together

1. Humanity Builds

Lunafilament research creates the reason for a moonbase-scale project.

2. Delphi Drives

Delphi is the corporate banner most closely tied to that technology push.

3. The Cradle Holds It

The Cradle is the lunar facility where the project and disaster physically live.

4. IDUS Breaks It

IDUS is the AI term that explains why that facility becomes a survival nightmare.

Important boundary:
Public sources are strong on the broad structure above, but not equally strong on every hidden systems detail or deeper spoiler-specific backstory. This page sticks to the high-confidence map.

IDUS & Delphi Explained FAQ

What is the Cradle in Pragmata? +

The Cradle is the massive lunar base where Pragmata takes place. Current official coverage treats it as the main moon facility tied to the Lunafilament crisis and Hugh and Diana's escape route.

What is Delphi in Pragmata? +

Delphi, or Delphi Corporation in current public coverage, is the corporate force most closely tied to Lunafilament, advanced generative AI, and the larger moonbase project behind the Cradle.

What is IDUS in Pragmata? +

IDUS is the rogue AI associated with the lunar research station crisis. Official launch messaging and review coverage both present it as the hostile artificial intelligence at the center of the station's collapse.

How do IDUS, Delphi, and the Cradle connect? +

The safest public reading is that Delphi is the corporation behind the larger moonbase project, the Cradle is the lunar facility where the game happens, and IDUS is the AI crisis that turns that facility into a survival problem.
